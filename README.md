## 📌 Project Title

**NutriNode – Healthy Nutrition on a Budget**

---

## 📖 Description

NutriNode is a web-based platform designed to help low-income families plan affordable, nutritious meals for everyday life. The project focuses on making healthy eating accessible by combining budget-friendly meal plans, simple recipes, and an intuitive weekly planner.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To bridge the gap between nutrition and affordability by providing practical tools that empower users to make healthier food choices without exceeding their budget.

---

## 🚀 Features

* 🥗 **Budget-Friendly Meal Plans** – Pre-designed meal plans optimized for low cost and high nutrition
* 📅 **Weekly Planner** – Plan meals efficiently for the entire week
* 📖 **Simple Recipes** – Easy-to-follow recipes using affordable ingredients
* 🌙 **Modern UI** – Clean, responsive design with dark mode support
* ⚡ **User-Friendly Interface** – Designed for accessibility and ease of use
